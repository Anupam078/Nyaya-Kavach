LAND PAPER

Budhni Bai, a tribal woman, of Kalapani village, gives her 2 acres of land (which is registered as Bhumiswami in her late husband's name) to Ramesh Agarwal for 10 years, in exchange for a lump sum of Rs. 15,000.

Ramesh Agarwal may do whatever he wants with this land, farming or anything else.

There is no mention of Collector's permission anywhere in this paper.
